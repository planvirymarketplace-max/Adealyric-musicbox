   227→   227→## 3. Sizing Guides
   228→   228→
   229→   229→Your data shows **four distinct size runs** across products — don't use one universal chart.
   230→   230→
   231→   231→| size_guide_id | Range seen | Used by |
   232→   232→|---|---|---|
   233→   233→| `women_std` | S – XL (some S–2XL, S–3XL) | Most tees, hoodies, sweatshirts |
   234→   234→| `women_numeric` | 4 – 12 | Yoga tanks, sports bras, some leggings (reads like girls'/juniors' numeric — verify with supplier before publishing) |
   235→   235→| `women_ext` | 2XS – XL | Color-block/inclusive-size lines |
   236→   236→| `pod_std` | XS/S – 3XL–8XL | All print-on-demand items (varies per style, always shown per-product) |
   237→   237→
   238→   238→**Recommended schema:**
   239→   239→```json
   240→   240→{
   241→   241→  "size_guide_id": "string",
   242→   242→  "sizes": ["2XS","XS","S","M","L","XL","2XL","3XL"],
   243→   243→  "measurements": {
   244→   244→    "S": { "chest_in": 34, "length_in": 26 },
   245→   245→    "M": { "chest_in": 36, "length_in": 27 }
   246→   246→    // populate per style once you have supplier spec sheets — not present in source data
   247→   247→  }
   248→   248→}
   249→   249→```
   250→   250→Your raw dump gives size **ranges** only (e.g. "S–XL"), not per-size chest/length measurements — you'll need to pull that from supplier spec sheets separately; it's not in the pasted catalog.
